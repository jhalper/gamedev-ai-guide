About This Tool

The Game Dev Job Search Guide is a companion to the Game Dev AI Guide. It helps game developers navigate the job search landscape with personalized recommendations.

Every recommendation is based on research into 15+ job platforms, 12+ AI tools, and current market data from GDC 2025-2026 and industry sources. No motivational fluff — concrete, honest, practical.

AI tools augment your search — they don't replace doing the real work. A referral from a former colleague is still worth more than 100 AI-optimized cold applications.

Created by HALP (Joe Halper)
